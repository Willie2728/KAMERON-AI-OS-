# KAMERON AI OS — Growth Run 19

## Campaign: Protect One Workload Before Standardizing Resilience

**Status:** Prompt Ready only. No media rendered. Nothing published.

## Customer truth

The adoption problem for enterprise resilience is not only whether checkpointing works. It is whether a buyer can test recovery on a workflow valuable enough to matter without committing the entire agent estate at once.

KAMERON's current launch packaging already supports a scoped paid enterprise pilot: 1–3 high-value workloads, recovery objectives, checkpoint/storage requirements, integrations, and success metrics such as recoverability, time saved, compute/API cost avoided, and operational continuity.

## Creative strategy

**Hook:** Don't start with every agent. Start with the workflow you cannot afford to restart from zero.

**Supporting idea:** Protect one high-value autonomous workload, interrupt it deliberately, validate the checkpoint and recovery policy, and measure whether trusted resume beats starting over.

**CTA:** Scope a KAMERON recovery pilot around one high-value workload.

## 5-panel LinkedIn carousel prompt

1. **ONE WORKLOAD YOU CANNOT AFFORD TO RESTART** — show a long-running autonomous workflow with accumulated context, compute, and approved progress.
2. **INTERRUPT IT ON PURPOSE** — outage / dependency failure / runtime interruption. No disaster theater.
3. **CHECKPOINT ≠ AUTOMATIC RESUME** — show integrity, policy, dependency, permission, environment, and fingerprint checks before recovery eligibility.
4. **RESUME FROM TRUSTED STATE** — continue only after the recovery gate approves the state.
5. **MEASURE THE PILOT** — recoverability, time saved, compute/API cost avoided, operational continuity. CTA: scope one high-value workload.

## Claims guardrails

- Do not claim zero downtime, zero data loss, universal recovery, or guaranteed savings.
- Do not present planned enterprise packaging as an active signed customer contract.
- Do not claim a workload can be protected without the required KAMERON runtime/SDK/adapter integration.
- Pricing and pilot structure remain governed by `docs/PRICING_AND_PACKAGING.md`.
