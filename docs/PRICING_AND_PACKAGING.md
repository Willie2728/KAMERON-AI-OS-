# KAMERON AI OS — Launch Pricing & Packaging

Pricing is designed around protected workers, checkpoint storage, retention, governance depth, deployment model and enterprise support. Model/API consumption and customer infrastructure are separate unless explicitly bundled.

## Developer — $299/month
Best for builders validating resilient agent workflows.
- 5 protected workers
- 25 GB checkpoint storage
- Python + Node SDK access
- 7-day recovery history
- Basic webhook/API integration
- Community/email support

## Team — $1,250/month
Best for production teams running business-critical automations.
- 25 protected workers
- 250 GB checkpoint storage
- 30-day recovery history
- Runtime supervisor
- Webhook/API connectors
- Basic recovery analytics
- Priority support

## Business — $4,500/month
Best for departments operating autonomous workflows at scale.
- 100 protected workers
- 2 TB checkpoint storage
- 90-day recovery history
- Hybrid storage options
- SSO/RBAC
- Advanced audit + recovery analytics
- AI SWARMER trust connector
- Priority architecture support

## Enterprise — Custom annual contract
Best for regulated, high-scale, private-cloud or on-premises deployments.
- Contracted/unlimited workers
- On-prem/private cloud deployment
- Custom retention and recovery policies
- Customer-owned storage options
- Premium SLA
- Dedicated architecture and deployment services
- Custom compliance/security integrations

## Paid enterprise pilot
Recommended launch offer: **$15,000-$40,000** for a scoped 6-10 week pilot.

Pilot should define:
- 1-3 high-value workloads
- Baseline restart/failure cost
- Recovery point objectives
- Checkpoint frequency and storage needs
- Integration requirements
- Success metrics: recoverability, time saved, compute/API cost avoided, operational continuity

A portion of the pilot fee may be credited toward the first annual agreement when converted.

## Packaging principle
Do not market KAMERON as cheap autosave. Price it against the value of work, compute, context and operational continuity that the customer cannot afford to lose.
